Tiny Supermarket Tycoon

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email yhtiyarachilov@icloud.com, and include your device (for example "iPhone 13") and what happened. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

Is my progress saved?
Yes, automatically, on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress. Progress does not move between devices.

Does the shop earn money while I'm away?
Yes, at half its recent pace, for up to 2 hours, as long as there is stock to sell. You'll see a "Welcome back" summary when you return.

My shelves are empty and shoppers leave.
Buy stock in the Shop, then restock shelves, or hire a stocker so shelves refill themselves. Watch the tips at the top of the screen.

How do I make my shop bigger?
From level 8, open Build mode and tap "Expand shop".

Does the game need internet or cost anything?
No. It's free, works offline and has no purchases.

How do I start over?
Pause → Start a new store. This erases your current progress.
