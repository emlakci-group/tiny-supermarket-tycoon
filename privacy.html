Privacy Policy

ShelfUp

Last updated: November 7, 2026

In short: ShelfUp does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. The game works fully offline and has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What the game stores

Your game progress (your shop, money, level and settings) is saved only on your own device, inside the app's private storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not connect to the internet, does not ask for an account, and does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos. It contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools.

Apple

If you have chosen to share diagnostics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. These contain no personal information. Apple's own handling of data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no information from anyone, we collect no information from children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example optional ads or cloud saves), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
